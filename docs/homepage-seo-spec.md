# Greece Taxi & Private Transfers — Homepage SEO Specification

## 1. Primary SEO Target

### Primary topic

**Private Taxi & Transfers in Greece**

### Primary search intent

Commercial / transactional.

The visitor should immediately understand:

* What the company does
* Where it operates
* What types of transfers are available
* That private transfers can be arranged throughout Greece
* How to request a quote

### Primary keyword theme

* Greece taxi
* taxi Greece
* private taxi Greece
* Greece private transfers
* Greece airport transfers
* private airport transfer Greece
* Greece taxi transfer
* private transfer Greece

Do **not** repeatedly stuff these exact phrases into the page. Use natural semantic variations.

# 17. FAQ Section

## H2

**Frequently Asked Questions**

Use approximately 6–8 questions.

### FAQ 1

**Do you provide private taxi transfers throughout Greece?**

Yes. Transfer services can be arranged for journeys between airports, cities, ports, hotels and destinations throughout Greece, subject to availability.

### FAQ 2

**Can I book an airport transfer in Greece?**

Yes. You can request private airport pickup or drop-off for major Greek airports and surrounding destinations.

### FAQ 3

**Do you provide transfers between Greek cities?**

Yes. Private city-to-city transfers can be requested for routes across mainland Greece and selected island destinations.

### FAQ 4

**Can I book a transfer from a Greek airport to a hotel?**

Yes. Airport-to-hotel transfers are one of the main services offered.

### FAQ 5

**Can you arrange transfers to cruise and ferry ports?**

Yes. Private transfers can be requested between airports, hotels, cities and major Greek ports.

### FAQ 6

**How far in advance should I book my transfer?**

Advance booking is recommended, particularly during Greece's busy summer travel period.

### FAQ 7

**How do I request a transfer quote?**

Use the quote form and provide your pickup, destination, date, time, passengers and luggage details.

### FAQ 8

**Can I request a custom transfer route?**

Yes. If your route is not listed, submit the journey through the quote form and provide the complete pickup and destination details.

# 34. Visual Design Direction

## Color palette — deliberately not the stereotypical black/gold or blue/white Greek look

Almost every Greek tourism website uses blue and white. This site uses a more premium Mediterranean / Aegean sunset palette instead.

| Purpose    | Color               | Hex       |
| ---------- | -------------------- | --------- |
| Primary    | Deep Teal            | `#123C43` |
| Secondary  | Mediterranean Teal   | `#176B70` |
| Accent     | Coral                | `#E8755C` |
| Highlight  | Warm Sand            | `#F3E7D3` |
| Background | Soft Ivory           | `#FCFAF5` |
| Dark Text  | Charcoal             | `#243033` |
| Muted Text | Slate                | `#667477` |
| White      | White                | `#FFFFFF` |

### Why this works

* Deep Teal gives a premium transportation feel.
* Coral becomes the CTA/conversion color.
* Warm Sand gives the website a Mediterranean feeling.
* Soft Ivory prevents the site from looking like another generic SaaS website.
* It deliberately avoids the conventional Greek blue/white aesthetic.

Visual direction: **Deep Teal + Ivory + Coral + Mediterranean photography** — not Navy + white + gold.

## Typography

A modern editorial combination, deliberately different from the Playfair/Inter luxury style used in an earlier black-and-gold concept.

* Headings: **DM Sans**
* Body: **Inter**
* Optional accent: **DM Sans Medium/Italic**

### Recommended type scale

| Element     | Desktop  | Mobile  |
| ----------- | -------- | ------- |
| H1          | 56–64px  | 38–44px |
| H2          | 40–46px  | 30–34px |
| H3          | 24–28px  | 21–24px |
| Body        | 17–18px  | 16px    |
| Small text  | 14px     | 14px    |
| Button      | 15–16px  | 15–16px |

Use generous line height. Don't make the homepage typography overly decorative — the visual personality should come from the layout, photography, cards and colors, not the type treatment.
